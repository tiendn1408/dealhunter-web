# Surface: Home (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Vietnamese online shoppers, non-technical, mostly on phones. Job: find out whether the thing they want is at a good price right now. Action: paste a marketplace product link and get the real current price, as a guest. Proof: only the visitor's own real result (no sample products, prices, counts or quotes exist). Constraints: real data only; affiliate "buy" offered, never pushed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The market price board. Every product is a cardboard price tag pinned to a pine chalkboard: old prices struck through in red marker, the new one written over them, which is exactly what DealHunter's real price history is. Refuses the category default of a white SaaS hero with a screenshot and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Pine chalkboard (#0A3832 family) as the ground of whole regions; chalk white ink; kraft cardboard tags (#D2A86A) for products and prices, pinned with a red tack; red marker strike for superseded prices; teal chalk (#3CCFB0) for the good price and the primary action. Hand-lettered marker face only for prices, strikes and board headlines; Be Vietnam Pro for everything read. Flat, square-cornered, no gradients, no shadows at rest, no glass.

STORY: The visitor recognises the market board at once, understands that DealHunter writes down the real price every time it checks and crosses out the old one, pastes their own link, watches their tag get written, and decides to keep tracking or buy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed pine board. Left: board headline in large hand lettering "Giá hôm nay, có nên mua?" filling the measure; one supporting sentence; the paste slot as a chalk-white strip on the board with a teal "Viết giá lên bảng" button. Right: an empty cardboard tag pinned with a red tack that becomes the visitor's real tag (title, struck old prices when history exists, current price in marker) once they paste.

FORM: Bảng giá chợ (handwritten market price board), position 6 of 7 on the ordered list. seed: none (unavailable).
Raises kept: every board band fills the measure (hatch show print); the tracked list packs tags edge to edge like a stall, the open one circled in marker (doujin catalog); the first price reading walks real fixed stations (darkroom); the board colour owns whole surfaces (indoor weather sun).
Signature interaction: paste a link and watch your own tag get written on the board, live, from the real API (link received, price read, history started).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
